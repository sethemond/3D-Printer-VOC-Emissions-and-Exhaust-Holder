## Key Technical Information

### Emissions Characteristics
- **UFP (Ultrafine Particles):** 11.5-116nm range
  - PLA: ~2.0×10^10 particles/min
  - ABS: ~1.9×10^11 particles/min

- **VOC (Volatile Organic Compounds):**
  - Styrene (ABS/HIPS): 10-110 μg/min
  - Lactide (PLA): 4-5 μg/min
  - Caprolactam (Nylon): 2-180 μg/min

### Filtration Solution
- **Pre-filter:** MERV 16 (captures 0.01-0.12 μm particles - 100% UFP coverage)
- **Main filter:** Granulated Activated Carbon (GAC) for VOC absorption
- **Design basis:** Prusa Air Manager V2 community design

### Sensor System
- **MOx Sensors:** SPG30/SPG40 (hotplate metal-oxide VOC detection)
- **Environmental:** SHT31 (temperature & humidity)
- **Architecture:** Arduino-based, 3-point measurement
  1. Baseline (room ambient)
  2. Enclosure interior
  3. Filter outlet

### Mechanical Design
- Custom 3D-printed mounting brackets
- Over-center clamping mechanism for filter attachment
- Print-in-place hinges
- Multiple iterations: V1, V2, V3
